Welcome

More than a course.
A season of formation.

The KLM Online School of Ministry was established in 2017 to provide life-development and ministry training for those who desire to deepen their knowledge of God’s Word and become more effective in life, leadership and ministry.

In August 2021 it launched its online learning platform, opening the programme to the international community and drawing students from six nations and beyond.

Programmes & Courses

Thirteen modules.
One deliberate curriculum.

The curriculum is taught across thirteen learning modules, covering the person of Christ, the gospel, the Scriptures, practical ministry, leadership, and the ordering of a believer’s life.
