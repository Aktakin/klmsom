Who We Are

Established in 2017, teaching a global classroom

The KLM Online School of Ministry was established in 2017 to provide life-development and ministry training for individuals who desire to deepen their knowledge of God’s Word and become more effective in life, leadership and ministry.

In August 2021 it launched its online learning platform, making the programme accessible to the international community. Students have since joined from the United Kingdom, the United States, France, South Africa, Ghana and Nigeria.

Vision & Mission

What we are here to do

Our Vision

To become a globally recognised online Christian training institution that develops spiritually mature, biblically grounded and transformational leaders for effective service in life and ministry.

Our Mission

To raise a generation of highly effective leaders who are grounded in God’s Word, equipped for ministry, and empowered to make a positive impact in the Church and society.

Training Philosophy

How we teach

Through sound biblical teaching, practical ministry training, leadership development and spiritual formation, the School equips believers with the knowledge, character and skills required to fulfil their God-given purposes and to make a lasting impact in their churches, communities and spheres of influence.

Active participation is expected from each student, to ensure everyone benefits from the class discussions.
