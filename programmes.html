Programme Overview

What the School teaches

Teaching is delivered across thirteen learning modules, covering the person of Christ, the gospel, the Scriptures, practical ministry, leadership, and the ordering of a believer’s life.

Classes hold only on Zoom, and active participation is expected from each student so that everyone benefits from the class discussions.

Guidelines for a Fruitful Learning Experience

  1. Mute your microphones when not speaking to minimize distractions.
  2. Use the “raise hand” icon to signify your desire to speak or ask questions.
  3. Active participation is expected from each student to ensure everyone benefits from the class discussions.
  4. Stable network connection: Please ensure you have a reliable internet connection to avoid disruptions. If your network is unstable, kindly use the chat box to communicate with us.
  5. Zoom etiquette: As our classes will be strictly on Zoom, please strive to maintain a stable connection to stay engaged with the lessons.

Course Structure

The thirteen
learning modules

Every student takes every module. Individual course pages can be added as the website expands.

01

Christology

Understanding the person, nature, and work of Jesus Christ.

02

The Gospel

The good news of salvation, grace, and eternal life.

03

The Bible

Discovering the inspired Word of God and its application.

04

Homiletics

The art and science of preparing and delivering sermons.

05

Church Administration

Principles and practices for effective church management.

06

Leadership Ministry

Developing leaders who inspire, serve, and transform.

Career, Influence & Success

08

Music Ministry

Using music and worship to glorify God and uplift hearts.

09

Finance & Biblical Economics

Stewarding resources wisely according to biblical principles.

10

Supernatural Ministry

Operating in the gifts and power of the Holy Spirit.

11

Prayer

Building a strong prayer life and communion with God.

12

Spiritual Authority

Understanding and exercising the authority believers have in Christ.
