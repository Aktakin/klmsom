PROPOSED WEBSITE FOR KLM ONLINE SCHOOL OF MINISTRY
INITAIAL WEBSITE DESIGN FORMAT

1. Home
The homepage should quickly answer: Who are we? What do we offer? Why should I enrol? How do I apply?
Recommended content:
- KLM Online School of Ministry
- Tagline: “Equipping Believers. Developing Leaders. Advancing the Kingdom.”
- Brief introduction to KLM School of Ministry
- Current/next cohort announcement
- Brief programme highlights
- Why study at KLM SOM?
- Featured courses
- Student/graduate testimonies
- Upcoming events
- Clear “Apply Now” and “Explore Programmes” buttons
2. About KLM School of Ministry
This page establishes the identity and purpose of the school.
Recommended content:
- About KLM School of Ministry
- Our Vision
- Our Mission
- Our Purpose
- Biblical foundation
- Training philosophy
- School objectives
- Message from the General Overseer
- Message from the Dean of Studies
- School leadership/faculty
- Brief history of the School of Ministry
3. Programmes & Courses
This should provide prospective students with enough information to understand what they will learn.
Recommended content:
- Programme overview
- Programme duration
- Delivery format
- Course structure
- Course/module titles
- Brief description of each course
- Learning outcomes
- Who should attend?
- Programme completion requirements
- Certification awarded
- “Apply for the Programme” button
Individual course pages can later be added as the website expands.
4. Admissions & Enrolment
This should be the website's major conversion page.
I recommend presenting the admission process visually as:
Discover → Apply → Review → Admission → Enrol → Begin Your Journey
Content should include:
- Admission requirements
- Who is eligible?
- Application opening/closing dates
- Programme commencement date
- Programme duration
- Fees, where applicable
- Application instructions
- Required applicant information/documents
- Online application form
- Application confirmation
- Enrolment/registration process
- Admissions FAQs
- Apply Now
- Check Application Status, if technically feasible
5. Testimonies & Impact
Rather than simply displaying quotations, this page should demonstrate the transformational impact of SOM.
Recommended content:
- Graduate testimonies
- Student experiences
- Video testimonies
- Ministry transformation stories
- Graduate achievements
- Impact statistics
- Cohort highlights
- Graduation photographs
Possible headline:
“Transformed to Transform Others.”
6. News, Events & Calendar
This keeps the website active and gives existing and prospective students reasons to return.
Include:
- Latest SOM news
- Admissions announcements
- Upcoming cohorts
- Orientation
- Special lectures
- Conferences/seminars
- Graduation ceremonies
- Alumni programmes
- Ministry events
- Photo/event highlights
The calendar could provide simple Upcoming Events cards rather than a complex calendar system initially.
7. Alumni & Ministry Deployment
This is particularly important because ministry education should ultimately lead to service and impact.
Recommended content:
- About the SOM Alumni Network
- Alumni success stories
- Where our graduates are serving
- Ministry deployment opportunities
- Volunteer/service opportunities
- Continuing ministry development
- Mentoring opportunities
- Alumni events
- Alumni registration/update form
- Join the Alumni Network
A strong message for this page could be:
“Equipped to Serve. Empowered to Lead. Deployed to Impact.”
8. Contact & Support
Keep this straightforward and accessible.
Include:
- Contact KLM School of Ministry
- Administrative enquiries
- Admissions enquiries
- Programme enquiries
- Telephone/WhatsApp contact
- Email
- Contact form
- Church/SOM location
- Office hours
- Social-media links
- Frequently Asked Questions
Recommended Main Website Menu
For Phase 1, I would keep the navigation especially clean:
HOME | ABOUT | PROGRAMMES & COURSES | ADMISSIONS | IMPACT | NEWS & EVENTS | ALUMNI | CONTACT
With a prominent contrasting button:
APPLY NOW
